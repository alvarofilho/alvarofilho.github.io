# Personal Site Content

The site's editorial content is published in Portuguese and English, with a distinct preview state for work that is not ready for public discovery.

## Language

**Post**:
A published article identified by its language and slug. A Post is eligible for public navigation, feeds, and search-engine discovery.
_Avoid_: article, published page

**Draft**:
An unpublished article preview. A Draft is available only through draft routes and is excluded from public discovery.
_Avoid_: unpublished post, private post

**Language Variant**:
A Post or Draft that represents the same editorial subject in another language and shares its translation key.
_Avoid_: translation, locale copy

**Canonical URL**:
The public URL selected as the preferred address for a page or Post. The Portuguese home URL is the site root, and existing public URLs remain stable.
_Avoid_: primary link, main route
